03. GROWING UP WITH PEOPLE

Family taught me what people mean to me.

My family has had one of the biggest influences on who I am. We were close and often spent time together, and even when conflicts happened, I saw people come back to each other and make peace.

I still remember my grandmother telling me, “결국 가족뿐이다.”

When I hear stories from people who are not close to their families, I realize how much love and support I have received from mine.

Because of my family, I naturally came to value relationships, love, and being able to both give and receive love.

04. PERSPECTIVES

Maybe there is more than one way to see things.

Living and studying in different environments made me realize that what feels normal to me may not feel normal to someone else. I learned that people can look at the same situation and understand it completely differently. Because of that, I don't want to decide too quickly that my way is the only right way.

OPEN-MINDEDNESS

Being open-minded does not mean that I have no standards.
It means that I am willing to look again.

05. AWARENESS

Some things are harder for me to ignore.

Through Stage 1, I realized that I have higher standards for myself than I had thought. I think about many different parts of life: family, health, relationships, my attitude, the way people treat each other, and even small things that other people might easily overlook.

Sometimes this makes me stressed. When I first came to Handong, I sometimes wondered why I could not simply take things as lightly as other students seemed to.

But I started to realize that this part of me is not only a weakness.

06. PROBLEM SOLVING

Problems make me want to do something.

When I notice a problem, I tend to focus on how it can be solved rather than simply accepting it. I have noticed this especially in relationships. When there is a conflict with someone, I feel frustrated when the problem is avoided instead of being talked about. Through these experiences, I realized that problem-solving is one of my strengths.

I know that I cannot solve every problem in the way I want.
But I would rather look for something I can do than know that something is wrong and choose to do nothing.
07. PEOPLE

What if I were them?

When I am part of a majority, it is easy to overlook someone who does not naturally fit into the group. But when I imagine being that person, I can understand how lonely or difficult it might feel. That makes me want to become someone who looks around instead of simply staying comfortable inside the majority.

I believe that before being good at something, we are human beings who affect and are affected by other people.

ABILITY MATTERS. BUT CHARACTER MATTERS TOO.
08. MALAYSIA

Then I met people whose lives were completely different from mine.

During a mission trip to Malaysia, I met stateless Filipino refugees who were living in difficult conditions. They lived on houses built over the water, connected by wooden paths, and had to live carefully and quietly.

“숨어서 살고 싶지 않다.”

I could not stop thinking about how two people can be born into completely different lives simply because of their parents, their birthplace, or the country they belong to.

I realized that success and comfort are not meaningless. But if I see someone suffering and choose to do nothing, I don't think my success would feel meaningful enough.

I want to use what I have to help people.

09 & 10. PRESENT & CREATION

Right now, I am learning.

Learning about health has made me think more seriously about how we live and take care of the bodies we have.

The Creator & Biology

The more I learn about life, the more I want to know the Creator. One of my professors explained that there is no useless part of the human body and that even small things in our bodies can have a purpose. Hearing this made me grateful for the life God created and made me want to learn more about it.

My interest in biology eventually led me to become interested in creation science and DUDUS — because I did not want my curiosity to stay only in my head. I wanted to learn, ask questions, and explore these ideas with other people.

11. VISION

Some dreams don't disappear. They wait.

When I graduated from high school in 2023, I was accepted into pharmacy and nursing programs, but I eventually chose not to continue with either path. For a while, I thought that dream had ended.

But this semester, studying biology and health has brought that interest back. Learning about the human body, disease, and how different systems work has made me realize that I still enjoy learning about life and health. At the same time, my mission experience made me want to use my future abilities for other people.

PHARMACY + SCIENCE + MISSION

I don't know yet whether pharmacy will become my final path. But I am becoming more interested in the idea of becoming a professional missionary who can use a specific area of expertise to serve people.

12. AMBITION

I don't want to just have dreams.

I want to be someone who takes what I care about seriously enough to act on it. I want to have enough passion and energy to turn the things I want into something real.

But I also don't want to believe that there is only one correct path. If I give my best and the path still changes, I don't want to see that only as failure.
Maybe another path is waiting for me.

14. PHILOSOPHY

I don't simply want a long life. I want a meaningful one.

I don't want to spend my life working until I lose my health, only to spend everything I have trying to get it back. I want to take care of what has been given to me and use my time well.

If I could look back at my life, I want to be able to say that I really lived.
That I loved my family.
That I cared about people.
That I used what I was given.
That I did not ignore the things that mattered to me.
And that I could stand honestly before God.
15. CONTINUATION

I don't know exactly who I will become.

I still don't know where pharmacy will take me. I don't know exactly what kind of professional I will become. I don't know which dreams will stay and which ones will change.

And I think that's okay.

Stage 1 did not give me a final answer about who I am. Instead, it gave me a clearer picture of the things that move me, the problems I notice, the people I care about, and the questions I keep asking.
